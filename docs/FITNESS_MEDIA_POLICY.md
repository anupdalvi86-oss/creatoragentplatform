# Fitness exercise media policy

On 2026-09-25, the product owner confirmed that the exercise demonstration videos from [free-exercise-db-with-videos](https://github.com/harshvishu/free-exercise-db-with-videos) may be used in this Creator Agent Platform fitness app. This is the rights basis for on-demand playback of the catalog's source-hosted videos in the fitness PWA. It is an owner authorization for this app, not an independent verification of the upstream video's chain of title.

The bundled exercise metadata retains the source exercise IDs and license notice. Posters and MP4s are referenced at their source URLs and are loaded only when needed. This authorization does not grant rights to bulk download, rehost, edit, or redistribute the video collection elsewhere; those actions require a separate decision. It also does not change YouTube or Instagram media and transcript rights rules. Creator-authored fitness programs and challenges still require administrator review before publication.

If the catalog source, video host, or intended media use changes, review the authorization again before changing the adapter or content security policy.
